# Sky Whale — coolest-art-01 entry

A colossal blue whale swimming through the sky above a city skyline
at dusk, as if the air were ocean. Warm sunset light on its belly,
deep teal shadows on its back, tiny lit windows below, clouds drifting
between the towers like underwater currents.

Why this piece, against the judge's criteria:

1. **It stops you.** A whale the size of a city, in the sky, is not
   something the eye slides past.
2. **The idea is fresh.** The sky treated as an ocean, the city as a
   seabed: a surreal scale inversion, not a familiar scene.
3. **Bold style.** One giant subject, warm orange against deep teal,
   a single dusk mood across the whole frame.
4. **Clean craft.** The whale's anatomy, the light and the city all
   render cleanly; no text, no artefacts.
5. **It reads small.** At 300 pixels the whale's silhouette and the
   sunset band still carry the whole idea.
